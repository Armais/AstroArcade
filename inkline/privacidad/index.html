Inkline privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-06-05

This Privacy Policy describes how Inkline ("the App", "we") handles information when you use it. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Anonymous account ID. When you use community features, we create an anonymous account via Firebase Authentication. This does NOT require your name, email, or phone number.
  • User-generated content. Levels you publish, the votes you cast, and any reports you submit are stored so the community features can work.
  • Usage & diagnostics. Aggregated, anonymous analytics (for example which screens are used and crash data) to help us improve the App.
  • Local game progress. Your progress, settings, currency and cosmetics are stored ON YOUR DEVICE and are not uploaded.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any other personally identifying information. We do not sell your data.

3. Third-party services

The App uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics) operated by Google. Their handling of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. If advertising is enabled in a future version, ad partners (e.g. Google AdMob) may process a device advertising identifier; this policy will be updated accordingly.

4. Children

Inkline is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only identifier created is a random anonymous ID with no personal data attached.

5. Data retention & deletion

Published levels and votes are kept until you request their removal. To request deletion of your anonymous account and associated content, contact us at the email below with your level code(s) or anonymous ID.

6. Security

Data transmitted to our backend is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to stored community data is restricted by security rules.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: championastroarcade@gmail.com
