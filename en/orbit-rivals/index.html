What it's about

Orbit Rivals is a 3D multiplayer party board game to play with friends. Play online with your friends or with players from around the world, or on one device with controllers and against bots. Up to four players per match.

Each round you roll two dice, pick your path at every fork and land on spaces that change everything. At the end of the round, a minigame decides who takes the Sparks: there are 100 3D minigames and none of them repeats in the same match. And if someone challenges you to a duel, it is settled with one of the 11 two-player minigames.

The year 2742: humanity is spread across stations, mines and jump ports in entire galaxies, and four rivals compete for the stellar Cores.

Minigames

  • 100 3D minigames of survival, racing, timing, memory, reflexes, physics, co-op, puzzles and chaos.
  • Practice any of them in Training and beat your high score.

Modes

  • Six boards with their own forks, and the Grand Circuit, which runs through all of them.
  • Race mode: whoever lands exactly on the 64-space finish wins.
  • Minigame tournaments with no board or dice, as a league or first to 3 wins.
  • Single-player story mode: seven chapters and three endings.

Characters

  • 11 cadets, each with their own ability.
  • Create your own character piece by piece from sixteen xenotypes.
  • Skins, 51 dances, victory effects and 500 emotes.

Online

  • Rooms with a code to play with friends, or Quick match by level.
  • Friendly or competitive matches, with trophies and eight arenas.
  • Cross-platform play and voice chat in the room.
  • You can mute, block and report any player.

Before you play

The browser version downloads about 185 MB the first time, so Wi-Fi is best.

On phones it plays in landscape. On Android you can install it from Google Play.

It's free. Everything you can buy is cosmetic with no randomness, and online play has its own privacy policy.
