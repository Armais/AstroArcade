About the studio

Astro Arcade is Alfonso and Armais, two video game enthusiasts. We make the games we'd love to find as players: free, with no loot boxes and with ads and purchases in moderation. We have our own engine and we release on the web, Android and Windows.

Orbit Rivals

3D party board game

A 3D multiplayer party board game: roll the dice, race across the board and beat your rivals in 100 minigames. Online with friends or with players from around the world, or on one device with controllers and against bots. It includes a seven-chapter story mode.

Cotton vs Ants

2D platformer

A jump-and-run platformer adventure: guide Polusan, a brave little cotton, through 7 levels full of ants, traps and 3 epic boss battles. Run, jump and drop like a rock with your super-gravity to crush ants and rescue your friends.
