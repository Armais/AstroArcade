Pinball Master privacy policy

Updated on 25 November 2026.

This policy explains what data Pinball Master, Astro Arcade's pinball game, uses in the browser and in the Android app.

What stays on your device

Your progress, stars, records and settings are saved on your own device (in the browser or, in the Android app, in its private storage). They never reach us. The app also keeps the technical log of the last two sessions, also only on the phone.

The online leaderboards

For the leaderboards, the game connects to Supabase, the service that hosts their database. If you choose a name for the lists (3 to 16 characters), we keep that name and a key that is also saved on your device, so that you are the same player next time. There is no email and no password.

When you submit a game, we keep its recording (the table, the seed and what you pressed at every moment), the points, the time, the balls lost and the date. The server replays the recording to check there's no cheating. Your name, points and games are shown on the lists and anyone who plays can see them, so don't use your real name if you don't want it to be seen.

The anonymous statistics

To tune the difficulty, the game sends one row for every table you start: which table it is, how it ended (beaten, lost or left), how long it took and how many balls were lost, with a random number that groups the rows of the same attempt. It carries neither your name nor anything of yours. You can turn them off in Options.

Like any server, Supabase receives your device's IP address when it connects. You can read its privacy policy.

What we use it for

Only to run the leaderboards, to remove games made by cheating and to tune the difficulty of the tables.

What we don't do

There are no ads and no purchases. We don't ask for your email, your location or your contacts, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone else. The Android app only asks for the Internet and vibration permissions.

The browser version

In the browser, the game is served by itch.io, which has its own privacy policy.

The Android app

The app is downloaded from Google Play. Google handles its own data (your Google account, the download and, if you have it turned on in your phone, crash and performance reports) under its own privacy policy. Google Play only gives us aggregate figures (installs, app crashes) that do not identify you.

How long we keep it and how to delete it

We keep your name and your games for as long as the leaderboards exist. If you want to see what we have about you, correct it or have it deleted, write to us at championastroarcade@gmail.com with your player name. What is on your device you delete yourself: «Borrar el progreso» (delete progress) in Options, the site data in your browser or, on Android, the app's data (or by uninstalling it). If we don't answer as you expect, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency or the authority in your country.

Data controller

Astro Arcade, the studio of Alfonso and Armais. Contact: championastroarcade@gmail.com.
