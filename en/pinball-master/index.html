What it's about

Pinball Master is a 3D pinball game in portrait, to play with your finger or the keyboard. Every table has its own theme and goal: beat it and you move on to the next one with your points.

The campaign goes through worlds of nine tables, from the Apprentice to the Dragon's Lair, in order of difficulty. The fourth and seventh tables of each world are checkpoints, and every table can also be played on its own.

90 tables

  • The Forge, the Fair, the Glacier, the Temple, the Submarine, Billiards, Chess, the Grand Prix...
  • And the Cipotegato of Tarazona, table 27, for the 27th of August.

Every table, its own twist

  • Shifting gravity, whirlpools, river currents, a shark that hunts the ball, chess pieces that move...
  • Bonuses that carry over: ball saver, multiball, extra ball and slow motion.

Three stars per table

  • One for beating it, one for not losing a ball and one for doing it in time.
  • Ten worlds that open one after another.

Online leaderboards

  • Lists for every world and every table, plus a daily challenge that is the same for everyone.
  • The server replays every game before accepting it: no cheating.
  • Tap a player on the list to watch their game exactly as they played it.

Before you play

It's in development: we add and tune tables every week.

It plays in portrait, on your phone or your computer. Your progress is saved in the browser: if you clear the site's data, it's gone.

It's free. The online leaderboards have their own privacy policy.
