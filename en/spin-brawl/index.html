What it's about

Spin Brawl is a free-for-all 3D spinning-top brawler. Four tops enter the stadium and the last one standing wins: you are out if you leave the edge or run out of spin, and after 35 seconds the edge starts closing in.

During the countdown you aim your launch and then, once in the stadium, you decide every second: dash to attack or to save yourself, anchor to take the hit and send it back, or fire your ability when it is charged.

1728 tops

  • Six rings, six cores, six tips and eight abilities, combined however you like in the Workshop.
  • Colour, finish, stickers and trail, piece by piece: they only change how it looks.

Eight abilities

  • Wind, Fire, Lightning, Ice, Rock, Shadow, Life and Magnet.
  • You can see them coming and block them by anchoring.

Fifteen stadiums and four modes

  • Walls, Cage, Pit, Ice Rink, Volcano, Merry-go-round, Portals...
  • Classic, Race, King of the Hill and 2 vs 2.
  • A 48-challenge campaign with a boss at the end of each chapter.

Online

  • Quick match, or a room with a code to play with up to three friends.
  • Bots fill the empty seats, so nobody is left waiting.
  • Levels, daily missions and trophy leagues.

Before you play

It's in development: a prototype we change every week.

It plays in portrait, on your phone or your computer. The first online match can take up to a minute to connect: the server sleeps when nobody is playing.

It's free and everything is earned by playing. Online play has its own privacy policy.
