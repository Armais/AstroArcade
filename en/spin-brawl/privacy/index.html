Spin Brawl privacy policy

Updated on 17 November 2026.

This policy explains what data Spin Brawl, Astro Arcade's spinning-top game, uses.

What stays on your device

Your progress, level, trophies, tops and settings are saved on your own device (in the browser). They never reach us.

Online play

To play online, the game connects to our match server, hosted on Render (in Frankfurt, in the European Union). It sends your player name, your top and its look, and what you do during the match. The other players in the room see your name and your top, so don't use your real name if you don't want it to be seen.

The server doesn't store any of it: the room lives in its memory while the match lasts and disappears when the last player leaves. There are no accounts, no email and no password.

Like any server, it receives your device's IP address when it connects. We use it, only in memory, to limit how many connections come from the same address. Render, as the server provider, has its own privacy policy.

What we use it for

Only to run online play and to protect it from abuse.

What we don't do

There are no ads, no analytics and no purchases. We don't ask for your email, your location or your contacts, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

The browser version

In the browser, the game is served by itch.io, which has its own privacy policy.

Your rights

If you have any question about your data, write to us at championastroarcade@gmail.com. If we don't answer as you expect, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency or the authority in your country.

Data controller

Astro Arcade, the studio of Alfonso and Armais. Contact: championastroarcade@gmail.com.
