What drives us

We want to make games that hook you because they're fun and that, along the way, train things that matter off the screen: memory, reflexes or thinking up a strategy.

  • No loot boxes

    No paying for a surprise. If something costs money, you know exactly what you get.

  • Ads and purchases in moderation

    We don't fill our games with ads or microtransactions. A game has to be worth it on its own.

  • Playing teaches too

    A good game trains memory, attention and decision-making. We design with that in mind.

  • Quality over speed

    Every idea is tested by playing before we call it done. If it isn't fun, it doesn't go in.

Who we are

Astro Arcade is two video game enthusiasts. We make the games we'd love to find as players, to share that passion with the community and with players all over the world.

Alfonso

Co-founder

I'm a software developer and, above all, a curious person, always looking for something new to learn. I've loved video games since I was a kid, and now I'm excited to be on the other side, turning ideas into worlds other people enjoy. Making games is my way of keeping on exploring.

Armais

Co-founder

I'm an aerospace engineer by training, I've been programming for eight years and, above all, I'm a gamer. I like making the games I'd love to find myself: the kind that let you switch off from the world and always ask for one more round.
