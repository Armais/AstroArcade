Privacy

Updated on 6 November 2026.

This page explains what data the astroarcade.es website handles.

What this website does

It uses no cookies, no analytics and no ads, and it has no forms: we don't ask you for any data. Fonts and images are served from our own server.

Hosting

As on any website, the server that hosts it logs technical data about each visit, such as the IP address, the date and the page requested, so that it works and for security.

Content from other companies

The trailers are YouTube videos. They don't load until you press the play button; then they come from youtube-nocookie.com and Google's privacy policy applies.

The browser games are served by itch.io inside the "Play" pages. When you open one of them, itch.io's privacy policy applies. Links to YouTube and itch.io take you to their websites, which have their own policies.

The games

Orbit Rivals has its own privacy policy, which explains what data online play uses.

Cotton vs Ants has its own too, covering its online leaderboard: Cotton vs Ants privacy policy.

So does Inkline: Inkline privacy policy.

Contact

The data controller is Astro Arcade. For any questions about your data, write to us at championastroarcade@gmail.com.
