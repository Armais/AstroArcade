Cotton vs Ants privacy policy

Updated on 6 November 2026.

This policy explains what data Cotton vs Ants uses in the browser, on Windows and on Android. It replaces the February 2025 version, which described Unity's services: the game no longer uses them.

What stays on your device

Your progress, your records and your settings are saved on your own device. They never reach us.

The online leaderboard

For the leaderboard, the game connects to Supabase, the service that hosts the leaderboard database. When it connects, it creates an anonymous user: a random identifier, with no email and no password. A key is saved on your device so that you are the same player next time.

If you choose a name for the leaderboard, we keep that name and your best score in each level, with their sum. The name and the scores are shown on the leaderboard and anyone who plays can see them, so don't use your real name if you don't want it to be seen.

To take your player to another device, the game creates a 9-character code that works only once and expires after 24 hours.

Like any server, Supabase receives your device's IP address when it connects. You can read its privacy policy.

What we use it for

Only to run the leaderboard and to remove scores made by cheating.

What we don't do

There are no ads, no analytics and no purchases. We don't ask for your email, your location or your contacts, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

The browser version

In the browser, the game is served by itch.io, which has its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it and how to delete it

We keep your name and your scores for as long as the leaderboard exists. If you want to see what we have about you, correct it or have it deleted, write to us at championastroarcade@gmail.com with your player name. If we don't answer as you expect, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency or the authority in your country.

Data controller

Astro Arcade, the studio of Alfonso and Armais. Contact: championastroarcade@gmail.com.
