What it's about

Cotton vs Ants is a jump-and-run platformer adventure: guide Polusan, a brave little cotton, through 7 levels full of ants, traps and epic boss battles. Run, jump and drop like a rock with your super-gravity to crush every ant in your way.

Natulia is the Earth reborn after the Great Extinction. Cottons live on the surface and ants underground, in peace... until the bull ants break the Root Pact and kidnap your friends. Only you can bring them home.

7 levels, 7 worlds

  • The forest of Natulia, where it all begins.
  • Myrmecium, an old village built on the ruins of a human city.
  • The Stone Nest, a colosseum of antlions that throw bombs at you.
  • The hanging gardens, the Royal Anthill, a red canyon and Mirmeleo's dunes.

3 epic bosses

  • Antiopy, who rules the Stone Nest.
  • The Queen: she charges from wall to wall and rains resin.
  • Mirmeleo, the winged antlion, who only falls if you jump on him.

Pure platforming

  • Super-gravity smash with an anime-style impact.
  • Spikes, fire, falling platforms, bombs, boxes and portals.
  • Checkpoints and 3 hidden crystals in every level.
  • Responsive controls that forgive a late jump.

Made for your phone

  • Big touch buttons, plus keyboard and controller support.
  • Full screen in landscape.
  • In 8 languages: English, Spanish, Italian, French, Portuguese, German, Arabic and Chinese.
