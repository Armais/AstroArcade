Cotton vs Ants 4.0.4 · November 14, 2026

A Ferris wheel in level 6

  • Level 6: the stretch of platforms and crates is now a Ferris wheel with four boards.
  • See where you land: when you fall fast, Polusan no longer drops so low on the screen, and in the air a shadow shows where you’ll land.
  • On touch screens the camera shows a bit more of the level.

Try it on Android before anyone else

Cotton vs Ants is in closed testing on Google Play, and anyone can join:

  1. Join the Astro Arcade testers group.
  2. With the same Google account, open the test page and become a tester.
  3. Install it from Google Play.

If you can, keep it installed for at least 14 days: Google needs that before the game can be released to everyone.
