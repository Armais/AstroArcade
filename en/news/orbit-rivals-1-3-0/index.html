Orbit Rivals 1.3.0 · November 23, 2026

Tusko arrives, plus invite links

  • Tusko, the Mighty Mammoth, cadet number 12. His Charge adds 2 to the roll, and anyone on the spaces he passes moves back 2. He brings 40 emotes of his own, with his voice: there are now 540.
  • Invite with a link: your friend goes straight into your room. On Android it works even if they have to install the game first. You can also share the podium image.
  • RINGS OF SATURN, rebuilt: hand-designed sections, a breather after every hazard and a calmer pace. No more impossible races.
  • BASEBALL, easier: the ball’s glow tells you how high it comes (red, high; green, middle; blue, low).
  • RHYTHM has its own music and a bit more leeway. ANCHOR LINES gives you more margin when jumping.
  • DEVOURER: if the lives run out, the slugs win, and the lives now show next to the scoreboard.
  • PATH: the camera always follows whoever is playing on your screen.
  • Calmer online play: 30 seconds to roll (it used to be 15). If someone goes idle, a bot takes their seat after 30 seconds.
  • Mouse in joystick minigames: drag with the left button; the right one is the action.
  • The first guided game explains Powers and your ability.
  • Redeem code: a new card in the Dark matter tab, also in the browser and on desktop.
  • Play locally without giving your date of birth: it’s only asked for online play, and we explain why.
  • Aurora’s dice are no longer hidden behind the railing. A player on itch.io told us.
