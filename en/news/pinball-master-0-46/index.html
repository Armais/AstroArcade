Pinball Master 0.46 · November 26, 2026

Now in English

  • The whole game in English, and you can change the language in Options.
  • Fixed camera: the table fills the screen on tall phones too.
  • Automatic pause when you switch windows. From there you can retry the table and adjust the music and effects.
  • When you lose you see how close you were to the goal and a tip for the table.
  • Tables by world, with a shortcut to each one and the “Missing stars” filter.
  • More controls: the flippers also work with the mouse and the controller triggers (A to launch), plus a hint on how to use them after your first launch.
  • The Android app, in closed testing on Google Play, with online leaderboards, vibration and Share.

Try it on Android before anyone else

Pinball Master is in closed testing on Google Play, and anyone can join:

  1. Join the Astro Arcade testers group.
  2. With the same Google account, open the test page and become a tester.
  3. Install it from Google Play.

If you can, keep it installed for at least 14 days: Google needs that before the game can be released to everyone.
